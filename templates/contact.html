Frequently Asked Questions

How long are my files stored?

All files are automatically deleted after conversion or after 24 hours, whichever comes first.

Is there a file size limit?

Yes, the maximum file size is 16MB. If you need to convert larger files, please contact us for assistance.

Do you offer batch conversion?

Currently, we only support single file conversions. Batch conversion is on our roadmap for future updates.

Is SharpNet free to use?

Yes, SharpNet is completely free for all users with no hidden costs or subscription fees.
