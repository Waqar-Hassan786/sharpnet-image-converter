Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2025

Introduction

At SharpNet ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you about how we handle your data when you use our image conversion service and your privacy rights.

Information We Collect

When you use SharpNet, we collect and process the following information:

  • Files you upload: In order to perform the conversion service, we temporarily store the files you upload. These files are automatically deleted after the conversion process is complete or after 24 hours, whichever comes first.
  • Usage data: We collect anonymous information about how you use our website, such as the types of conversions performed, browser type, and operating system. This data is used for statistical purposes and to improve our service.
  • Cookies: We use essential cookies to ensure the proper functioning of our website. These cookies do not collect any personal information.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To process your file conversions
  • To improve and optimize our service
  • To detect and prevent technical issues

File Storage and Security

Your uploaded files are stored temporarily on our secure servers. All files are automatically deleted after conversion or after 24 hours. We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to help us operate our service, such as:

  • Web hosting services: To host our application and process your requests
  • Analytics services: To analyze usage patterns and improve our service

These third parties may have access to your data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

Advertising

We may use third-party advertising services to display ads on our website. These services may use cookies and similar technologies to collect information about your visits to our site and other websites to provide you with relevant advertisements.

Your Rights

Under certain circumstances, you have the following rights:

  • The right to access any personal information we hold about you
  • The right to request the deletion of your personal data
  • The right to object to processing of your personal data
  • The right to data portability

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@sharpnet.com
